Ask Udyogi

Got a question? Ask it here.

What is Udyogi?

Udyogi is a mobile app that connects people who need daily help with workers looking for jobs, such as cleaning, helping, cooking, driving and construction.

How do I download the app?

Tap any “Download on Google Play” button on this page, or search for Udyogi on the Google Play Store on your Android phone.

Is it free to use?

The app is free to download. Employers choose the pay for each job, and some features, such as subscriptions and platform fees, may apply when jobs are posted and completed.

How does a worker find a job?

Create a profile, browse jobs near you by category, check the pay and timing, and apply. The employer will accept or reply through the app.

How do I hire someone?

Post a job with the work details, time and the amount you want to pay. Workers apply, and you pick the one you trust.

How do I know a worker is trustworthy?

Check their profile and ratings from earlier jobs, and chat with them in the app before you confirm.

How are payments handled?

Online payments for fees and subscriptions are handled securely through Razorpay.

Which languages are supported?

You can pick your language when you first open the app, so it feels natural to use.

Is there an iPhone version?

Udyogi is available for Android on Google Play first. Other platforms may follow.
